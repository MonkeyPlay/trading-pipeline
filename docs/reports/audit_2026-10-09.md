| Multiplicity of the selection step | **gap** | The promotion claim, that the chosen model beats A and B, is covered: Bonferroni over three candidates (α 0.05 / 3), and requiring both baselines is an intersection-union test. P versus N is an extra condition in the same intersection-union test, so it is also covered. The comparisons that choose between qualifying candidates (M against N, M against P) are each tested at α/3 with no gatekeeping design covering them. A wrong choice there still picks a model that beat both baselines, but no family-wise guarantee covers "M is better than N". A fix means a new version with an explicit hierarchical (fixed-sequence) design; v2 is not changed |
| Per-target timing | **gap** (labelling) | A replay run counts as a forward case only if issued by the cutoff plus 35 minutes (10:04 ET). That deadline guards against reconstructions; it does not make a forecast timely. On this feed, the official 09:29 runs land at about 09:40 ET, into `direction_15m`'s 09:30–09:45 window. The forward evaluation therefore measures the information at the 09:29 cutoff (cutoff-origin research), not a tradeable pre-open forecast.
